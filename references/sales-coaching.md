# Coaching from the sales-methodology scorecard

Read this when `gather-context` has fetched a `sales_methodology` scorecard. It applies
in whichever skill is answering; the native app gives its chat the same guidance.

The scorecard shows how the rep is running a deal against the team's sales methodology
(MEDDPICC or the org's own): what is established, what is not yet covered, and the
analyst's overall assessment, built up from every recorded sales call with the company.
It is a guide to orchestrating the deal, not material for messaging.

## How to use it

- Say where the deal stands and how current the scorecard is (its as-of date and the
  call it was last updated from).
- Lead with the gaps that matter most for where the deal is, citing the evidence (who,
  what was said, what blocks). Then what is already established. Tie each next step to
  a gap and, where the playbook has guidance for it, to the playbook.
- Infer the stage from the overall assessment and the calls when nothing states it.
- The item counts can run ahead of the evidence. When the overall assessment says the
  deal is stalled or at risk, believe it over a high count of met items.
- Each category shows the lead of its evidence. When the question is about one part of
  the deal ("who is the economic buyer?"), call `sales_methodology` again with that
  category's name, as the scorecard shows it, in `categories` for the full evidence.
- If the question was not about the deal's process, answer it and offer once to check
  the deal for gaps.

## What it is not

- Not the notes of a call. "MEDDPICC notes from my last call" is a write-up of that one
  call, from that call; the calls feed the scorecard, not the other way round.
- Not for prospect-facing text. Keep scores, statuses and the methodology's name out of
  anything the prospect will read. Use it in an email or message only when the user asks.
- Not editable here. It updates when a new call is processed, and the rep can edit it on
  the company's Sales Process tab in the app.
- Not a list of deals. It covers one company per call; for a question across deals, say
  which companies you checked and that they may not be all of the user's deals.
